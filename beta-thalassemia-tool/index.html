📋 Disease Information

Beta Thalassemia

Description: Beta thalassemia is an inherited blood disorder characterized by reduced production of hemoglobin beta chains, leading to anemia of varying severity.

Gene: HBB (Hemoglobin Subunit Beta)

Chromosomal Location: 11p15.4 (Chromosome 11, short arm, position 15.4)

Inheritance Pattern: Autosomal Recessive

Clinical Classifications:

β-Thalassemia Major

Type: Transfusion-Dependent

Genotype: β⁰/β⁰ or β⁰/β⁺

Symptoms: Severe anemia, growth retardation, bone deformities, hepatosplenomegaly

Treatment: Regular blood transfusions, iron chelation therapy

β-Thalassemia Intermedia

Type: Moderate

Genotype: β⁺/β⁺ or β⁺/β⁰

Symptoms: Moderate anemia, splenomegaly, occasional transfusions needed

Treatment: Occasional transfusions, folic acid supplementation

β-Thalassemia Minor

Type: Asymptomatic Trait

Genotype: β/β⁺ or β/β⁰

Symptoms: Usually asymptomatic, mild microcytic anemia

Treatment: Usually none required
